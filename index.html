Charbel Toumieh

I develop real-time model predictive control and optimisation for agile aerial robots, from fast, decentralised swarms to bird-scale flapping-wing drones, and I validate it in flight. I am a postdoctoral fellow and scientist at the Laboratory of Intelligent Systems, EPFL, with Dario Floreano, and principal investigator of an Armasuisse project on vision-based aerial swarms.

Research

Aerial swarms

How can many drones fly fast through cluttered, unknown space without a central planner? Each drone runs model predictive control over time-aware safe corridors: convex regions of free space that keep it clear of obstacles, of space it has not yet seen, and of its neighbours.

Flapping-wing flight

Bird-scale flapping-wing drones are quiet and safe to fly near people and wildlife, but their lift, airspeed and turning are coupled through a few periodic inputs. I build models of these couplings compact enough to optimise over in real time, so that these aircraft can track agile trajectories accurately.

Papers

  • SwarmNxt: open-source software–hardware platform for fast and agile aerial swarms

    IEEE International Symposium on Safety, Security, and Rescue Robotics (SSRR), 2026, accepted

    An open platform for physical swarm research: assembly instructions, automated parallel software deployment, and a single ROS 2 stack for perception, planning and control.

  • Flying together: human-guided immersive shared control for aerial robot teams in unknown environments

    IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), 2026

    A virtual-reality framework in which an operator guides a team of drones through unknown, constrained environments in real time, while a planner keeps their flight feasible.

  • Accurate trajectory tracking with model predictive contouring control for bird-scale flapping-wing MAVs

    IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters, 2026

    A compact nine-state model identified from flight data enables what is, to our knowledge, the first model predictive contouring control of bird-scale flapping flight.

  • High-speed motion planning for aerial swarms in unknown and cluttered environments

    IEEE Transactions on Robotics, 2024

    The first decentralised, synchronous planner that lets every drone in a swarm fly at high speed while staying safe from other agents and from unexplored space.

  • Adaptive morphing of wing and tail for stable, resilient, and energy-efficient flight of avian-inspired drones

    npj Robotics, 2024

    A controller for an avian-inspired drone with eight degrees of freedom that stays stable through turbulence and actuator loss, and finds energy-saving wing and tail shapes in flight.

  • Decentralized multi-agent planning for multirotors: a fully online and communication latency robust approach

    arXiv preprint, 2023

    Makes the time-aware corridor planner fully online and adapts its planning frequency to communication delays.

  • Decentralized multi-agent planning using model predictive control and time-aware safe corridors

    IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters, 2022

    Introduces time-aware safe corridors, which encode other agents’ future positions as constraints in a real-time, decentralised MPC planner.

  • Voxel-grid based convex decomposition of 3D space for safe corridor generation

    Journal of Intelligent & Robotic Systems, 2022

    Decomposes the free space of a voxel grid into overlapping convex polyhedra, which turn collision avoidance into linear constraints for trajectory optimisation.
